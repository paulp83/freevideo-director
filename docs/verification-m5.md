# M5 verification — finishing (enhance, cut, export) on real ffmpeg

M5 STEP 4. The acceptance suite is `tests/test_finishing_acceptance.py` (22 tests, ~21 s on this
machine). It runs the real `assemble()` and real `ffmpeg`/`ffprobe` (6.1.1) over a synthetic
fixture film; only the GPU backends (SeedVR2, RIFE) and ComfyUI itself are stubbed. Everything the
test claims as measured below was observed by running it; numbers are from the 2026-10-21 run.

| Project state, jobs store, cut-plan fingerprinting | **real** code against a temp `PROJECTS_ROOT` (`/tmp/director-m5-accept-*`, removed at teardown) |

## What a full check needs on a real backend (not runnable here)

1. Enhance with SeedVR2 actually installed, upscale ON — check `decode_tiled=True`,
   `batch_size=4n+1`, and that `video.artifacts/rgb.npy` + `audio.wav` appear.
2. Enhance with RIFE installed and fps_policy=interpolated — frame count doubles in the graph run.
3. A film of real takes with mixed source fps — confirm `assemble` normalises without audio drift.
4. Listening check on the finished film (the numeric A/V check covers sync; it cannot catch
   content-level glitches).
5. VHS_VideoCombine output when the project fps is not 24 — golden captures are 24 fps only.

## Bugs the suite found

- **`xfade` negotiates `yuv444p`**: even with both inputs declared `yuv420p`, an `xfade` chain
  hands the encoder 4:4:4, so film exports were H.264 High 4:4:4 — QuickTime, browsers and TV
  hardware players reject them. Reproduced with a minimal ffmpeg 6.1.1 chain; the concat (no
  transition) path stays yuv420p. Fix: `project.assemble()` now passes `-pix_fmt yuv420p` on the
  output (restores the flag the M4 golden already pinned); the golden test is exact byte-equality.
- Test-side only: amplitude-envelope beep detection was wrong because `loudnorm`'s dynamic gain
  lifts the quiet segment at a scene join; replaced with a 1 kHz one-bin DFT tone-energy locator.

## Limitations of the suite

- Film assemble cost scales with the film, not with upscaling: the 3-scene 14 s fixture exports in
  4.6 s (24 fps) / 7.1 s (48 fps) at 1080p crf 16 `medium`; a full film's exports scale linearly
  with film length even with `upscale=off`.
- The golden parity tests pin the *canonical JSON* form (`{{ROOT}}`, `TIMESTAMP` normalization) of
  M4's outputs, not raw bytes of historical files; they prove the command/graph/plan shape is
  unchanged, not that old on-disk outputs are re-exported bit-identically.
- The beep tolerance is 40 ms per the brief; measured 8 ms comes from AAC priming plus the tone
  gate, not from xfade drift.
